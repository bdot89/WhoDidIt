DopingControl, built into WhoDidIt
==================================

This folder is DopingControl, the raid consumables / buffs / enchants checker by
ShempError: https://github.com/ShempError/DopingControl

All credit for it goes to its author. It is MIT licensed (see LICENSE in this
folder, which applies to it). WhoDidIt ships it at the commit named in
wdi_version.lua, with only the small changes needed to run it from inside
WhoDidIt (see tools\EmbedUpdate.ps1).
