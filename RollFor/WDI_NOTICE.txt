RollFor, built into WhoDidIt
============================

This folder is RollFor, the master-loot roller by Obszczymucha, in sica42's
1.12 fork: https://github.com/sica42/roll-for-vanilla

All credit for it goes to its authors. WhoDidIt ships it at the commit named in
wdi_version.lua, with only the small changes needed to run it from inside
WhoDidIt (its version comes from wdi_version.lua, its images are found in this
folder, its library lists are expanded into WhoDidIt.toc; see
tools\EmbedUpdate.ps1). Nothing in how it rolls is changed.

Its repository states no licence. This folder is not covered by WhoDidIt's MIT
licence, and it will be removed from WhoDidIt if its authors ask.
